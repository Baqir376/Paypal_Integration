Privacy policy

Last updated November 6, 2026

Salix is a hackathon project that helps sales teams review and practise sales calls. This page explains what Salix collects, why, and who it is shared with.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in with Google: your name, email address, Google account ID and profile picture link. If you sign up with email: your name, email address and a securely hashed password. Salix never sees your Google password.
  • Team details. Your team name, your role, and the email addresses of people you invite.
  • Call content. Recordings you upload, and the transcripts, scores, coaching plans and practice sessions created from them.
  • Payments. PayPal or Braintree handles payment details. Salix keeps only the order ID, the amount and the credits granted. It never receives or stores card or bank details.
  • Sign-in cookie. One cookie keeps you signed in for up to 30 days. Salix uses no advertising or analytics cookies and no tracking.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your team's data separate from other teams.
  • To analyse your calls and produce scores, findings, coaching plans and practice sessions.
  • To process credit purchases.

Salix does not sell your data, show ads, or use your data for anything beyond running the service.

Who processes your data

To provide the service, call content is sent to AI providers for processing: Google (Gemini) for analysis, Deepgram for transcription, and Groq as a backup model provider. Payments go to PayPal or Braintree. Each provider handles data under its own privacy policy.

Google user data

Salix requests only your basic Google profile: name, email address and profile picture. It uses this only to create and sign in to your Salix account. It does not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data, and does not share Google user data with anyone except as needed to sign you in.

Keeping and deleting data

Your data is kept while your account is in use. To have your account and its data deleted, email i230098@isb.nu.edu.pk from the address you signed up with.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
