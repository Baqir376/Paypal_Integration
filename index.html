Salix listens to the calls you can't.

An AI sales coach for B2B sales teams.

Salix scores sales calls in English, Urdu and Roman Urdu, quotes the transcript as evidence, writes a coaching plan for each rep's weakest skills, and lets reps practise against an AI buyer built from objections in real calls. Credits are paid through PayPal.

Watch the demoView the code

Built for the PayPal AI Hackathon. Payments run in PayPal's sandbox, so no real money moves.
